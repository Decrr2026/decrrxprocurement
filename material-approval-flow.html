DECRR Interio · Process Reference

From site shortfall to approved Purchase Order

Today, anyone with access to Purchase Orders can create and place one straight away — there's no checkpoint. This topology adds one: every material request a site raises has to clear a single senior approver before it becomes a Purchase Order in DECRR Interio.

No new screens needed. This runs entirely on the app as it exists today — the request and approval happen outside the software (a shared register, a WhatsApp note, a call), and the only trace left inside DECRR Interio is the Purchase Order itself, with the approval reference written into its Remarks / Notes / Terms & Conditions field.

The approver in this version is fixed — one senior person clears every request, for every site — so there's no routing logic to maintain, only a habit to enforce.

The flow

Five stages, one decision point, two ways back out. The dashboard already computes the trigger; everything from the second box onward is the new checkpoint.

Who does what

Three roles, none of them new hires — this is a discipline, not an org chart change.

Raises the request

Site In-charge

Watches the Dashboard's Purchase Required flag for their site, then logs a request: material, quantity, and why it's needed now rather than next cycle.

Clears the request

Senior Approver

One person, fixed, for every site. Checks the request against stock on hand, what's already on order, and the material's preferred vendor and rate before deciding.

Places the order

Procurement

Once approved, creates the Purchase Order in DECRR Interio as usual — and notes the approval reference in the order's Terms field for the record.

Request status

A request only ever sits in one of these four states.

Pending Approval Approved Changes Requested Rejected
Pending Approval — sitting in the senior's queue, untouched since the site raised it.
Approved — cleared to become a Purchase Order; procurement creates it referencing this approval.
Changes Requested — sent back with a comment (wrong quantity, missing reason, wrong material); the site edits and resubmits, re-entering Pending.
Rejected — closed for good, with a reason on file; no Purchase Order follows.

Running it day to day

No new software — just where each step happens inside what already exists.

  1. Spot the shortfall

    On the Dashboard's Procurement table, filter Stock Level to Purchase Required. That row is the trigger — Available Stock plus what's already on order can't cover the site's requirement.

  2. Log the request

    The site adds one line to the shared request register (a sheet, a channel, whatever the team already checks): material, quantity, site, and the reason. Give it a short reference, e.g. MR-LKO-014.

  3. Senior reviews against the app

    Before deciding, the senior opens Materials to confirm the Purchase Rate and Preferred Vendor, and Inventory to double-check nothing's already inbound for that site.

  4. Decision recorded on the register

    Approved, sent back for changes, or rejected — the outcome and any comment go back on the same line the site logged, so the request never loses its own history.

  5. Purchase Order created, referencing the approval

    Only after approval does anyone open Purchase Orders in DECRR Interio. The order's Terms field carries the reference, e.g. Approved: MR-LKO-014 by R. Sharma, 21 Sep — so the paper trail and the system record line up.
